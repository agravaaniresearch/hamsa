Hamsa

Hamsa filters Hacker News before any model sees it. Stories and comments are cleaned and scored on this computer against your own interests; a model is called only when you ask for a summary, with your key.

Hamsa needs permission to run on news.ycombinator.com before it can filter pages.

Appearance

Auto follows your system setting. The header on Hacker News and on the digest also has a button that cycles through the three.

Reading

Hamsa owns the whole reading layout on Hacker News, in the spirit of Modern for Hacker News. Fonts are bundled, so they work offline.

The quick brown fox: a sample of the text and title fonts you picked.

Reading log

Hamsa remembers the stories, threads and links you open, and the stories it showed you, in this browser only. What you open becomes a signal (sites, title words and tags you click), and the export is a ready-made dataset for tuning a local model on your own choices.

Interest sources

Each source only has to supply titles, links and, where it has them, tags. Tags feed the relevance graph; links, favourites and history feed the site and title signals. Nothing here leaves this browser except the calls to Raindrop and HN.

Raindrop
Create a test token in Raindrop under Settings, Integrations, For developers. Saving summary points back to Raindrop uses the same token.
HN favourites and upvotes
Favourites are public. Upvoted stories are read only if you are logged in to HN in this browser. This reads your HN pages, so it may stop working if HN changes its layout.
Browser bookmarks
Folders act as tags, so a folder called Oncology works like a tag. No key is needed and nothing leaves the browser. Firefox asks for permission the first time.
Browser history
Opt-in. Only which sites you read often over the last 90 days is kept, as a small signal. It stays in this browser.
Import a bookmarks file
Any browser's exported bookmarks (HTML), or a JSON list from Pinboard or another service. Folders and tags become tags.

Strongest interests

Tap a tag to boost it, tap again to mute it, once more to reset. Muted tags push matching stories and comments down.

Tags that are not topics, and extra instructions
Format labels such as essay, news or longform, and years, are already ignored.

Blocklist

A plain, shareable file of topic groups. A match never deletes a story: it moves it to toss with the rule written next to it, and a strong match on your own tags overrides the block. Drafted and suggested groups stay off until you switch them on.

Drafting is one short call to your selected model. Testing reads pages 1 to 4 of HN and shows what each group would have blocked; nothing is changed.

Scoring

Every story and comment gets a score from 0 to 1: the points of each signal are added up, then scaled with 1 - exp(-2 × total ÷ ceiling). These are starting values, to be tuned on real threads.

Providers

Bring your own keys. Keys stay in this browser and are sent only to the provider you add them for. Test a key to load its models, then tick the ones you want in the Summarise menu.
