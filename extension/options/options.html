Hamsa settings

Providers

Bring your own keys. Keys stay on this computer and are sent only to the provider you choose. Test a key to load its models, then tick the models you want in the sidebar.

Interests from Raindrop

Hamsa reads your bookmarks and tags once, builds a graph of what you care about, and ranks comments against it. Create a test token under Raindrop settings, Integrations, For developers. Saving points back to Raindrop needs the same token.

Strongest interests

Tap a tag to boost it, tap again to mute it, once more to reset. Muted tags push matching comments down.

Tags that are not topics

One per line. Format labels such as essay, news or longform and years are already ignored. Resync after changing this.
